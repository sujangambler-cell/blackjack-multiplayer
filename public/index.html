HOW TO PLAY — POKER

Texas Hold'em · best five · win the pot

Buy-in & your bank

The host sets the table buy-in. When you sit, that amount moves from your Casino X bank onto the table. The rest stays in your bank. All-in only risks your table stack — you stay seated; others can call, raise, or fold.

Auto-rebuy

Turn on Auto-rebuy in Settings. If you bust, the table buy-in is taken from your bank again (or granted if the bank is empty).

Bots

Hosts can tap the 🤖 button next to Host controls and add 1–5 bots. They use the same buy-in stack.

Flow of a hand

  1. Preflop — 2 hole cards each. Betting starts.
  2. Flop — 3 community cards.
  3. Turn — 1 more card.
  4. River — final card. Last betting round.
  5. Showdown — best hand wins (or last player standing).

Actions

  • Fold — give up the hand.
  • Check — pass when nothing is owed.
  • Call — match the current bet.
  • Bet / Raise — put more in; others must respond.
  • All-In — bet your entire table stack. You are still in the pot.

Hand rankings (best → worst)

  1. Royal Flush — A K Q J 10, same suit
  2. Straight Flush — five in a row, same suit
  3. Four of a Kind
  4. Full House
  5. Flush
  6. Straight
  7. Three of a Kind
  8. Two Pair
  9. One Pair
  10. High Card

Join mid-hand → spectate until the hand ends, then you can buy in if a seat is free.

HOW TO PLAY — BLACKJACK

Beat the dealer · don't bust · 21 is king

Goal

Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over.

Card values

  • Number cards = face value
  • J, Q, K = 10
  • Ace = 1 or 11 (whichever helps)

Your actions

  • Hit — take another card
  • Stand — keep your total
  • Double — double the bet, take exactly one card
  • Split — pair → two hands (when offered)

Dealer

Hits on 16 or less, stands on 17+.

Blackjack

Ace + 10 on the first two cards pays 3:2 (if the dealer does not also have blackjack).

Join mid-round → spectate until the next betting phase, then take a seat. Use the glowing ? anytime for this guide.
